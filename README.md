## Project

The goal of this project aims to help Local Bike optimize its sales and maximize its revenue thanks to the insights gained.


## Local Bike

Local Bike was founded by Alexander Anthony, a former professional cyclist who competed in major international races, including the Tour de France. During his training years in Europe, he experienced cities where cycling was deeply integrated into daily urban life. Upon returning to the United States, he noticed a significant lack of cycling infrastructure and culture in major metropolitan areas.

In 2016, Alexander retired from professional cycling to pursue a new mission: democratizing bike usage in the U.S. His goal was to promote sustainable mobility by encouraging urban residents to transform the way they commute, improve their health, and reduce their carbon footprint.

The name Local Bike reflects the company’s community-centered origins. Alexander began by organizing free bike repair workshops in his Brooklyn neighborhood to build an inclusive cycling community. When he opened his first store, he chose a name that embodied proximity, trust, and belonging—a place where both beginners and experienced cyclists feel at home.

Local Bike goes beyond selling bicycles. The company actively builds communities around sustainable transportation and promotes a vision of a future where cycling is central to everyday American life.


Local Bike has three stores: 

* Santa Cruz, California: An iconic outdoor town, ideal for road and mountain biking enthusiasts.
* Baldwin, New York: Strategically located on Long Island to serve families and commuters near New York City.
* Rowlett, Texas: A fast-growing city near Dallas with strong potential for developing cycling culture.

## Project

The goal of this project aims to help Local Bike optimize its sales and maximize its revenue thanks to the insights gained.

Local Bike has three locations: 

* Santa Cruz, California: An iconic outdoor town, ideal for road and mountain biking enthusiasts.
* Baldwin, New York: Strategically located on Long Island to serve families and commuters near New York City.
* Rowlett, Texas: A fast-growing city near Dallas with strong potential for developing cycling culture.
